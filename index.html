03Industrial Vision & Process Intelligence

Problem

Continuous plant video carried the process record, but nothing structured came out of it — no timings, no compliance data, no audit trail.

Built

End-to-end vision stack: dataset design and annotation pipelines, YOLO (v8/v11, OBB) and RF-DETR detector training and benchmarking, multi-object tracking (ByteTrack) with multi-frame debounce state machines, and temporal event grounding that converts noisy detections into validated, timestamped process events with evidence clips. Applications built on it: melt-cycle event tracking (heat start, tapping, deslagging, heat end), pour and mould tracking, PPE and restricted-area monitoring, casting visual inspection, multi-camera site deployments.

Impact

<3% false-trigger rate on production streams; >95% accuracy on compliance detection; 3–5 concurrent 1080p @ 25fps streams per device.

Stack

PyTorch · YOLO/OBB · RF-DETR · ByteTrack · DeepStream · TensorRT · OpenCV

04Manufacturing Optimization

Problem

Charge-mix and production decisions were made on operator intuition, leaving cost and capacity on the table.

Built

MILP charge-mix optimizer over grade chemistry windows, material recovery, inventory and furnace constraints, with Bayesian coefficient learning from realized heats and chance-constrained robustness. Separate multi-stage production planning optimizer across demand, line/furnace capacity and shift availability.

Impact

2–5% melt-cost reduction; 5–8% effective capacity unlocked with improved OTIF.

Stack

PuLP · Pyomo · Python · Bayesian inference · Operations research
