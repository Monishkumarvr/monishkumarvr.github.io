Melt-shop events from camera video

computer vision · edge · production

  1. camera
  2. detect
  3. track
  4. debounce
  5. event + clip
Problem
Brownfield melt shops record furnace events by hand, if at all, so there is no reliable time for when a heat started, tapped or was deslagged.
What I built
Cameras over the furnaces feed edge inference. Detection and tracking run per stream, and a debounce step turns noisy frames into one timestamped event — heat start, tapping, deslagging — with a short clip as evidence.
What changed
Over 95% accuracy on compliance detection, and false triggers under 3% on production streams.

Shipping models to a fleet of edge boxes

Jetson Orin · TensorRT · rollback

  1. build
  2. review
  3. activate
  4. health check
  5. rollback
Problem
Models have to reach edge boxes at five plants with nobody from the team on site, and a bad build must not take a camera down.
What I built
A review-gated deploy path to Jetson Orin boxes at five plants: the model is built into a TensorRT engine, activated, and watched by a health check that rolls it back on its own if it fails. The figure above plays this out.
What changed
Test to production went from about 3 days to 1, and a faulty build rolls itself back.

A plant assistant that reads live data

MCP · tool calling · governance

  1. question
  2. plan
  3. 7 sources
  4. check
  5. answer + links
Problem
The answers people need sit across seven data sources, from ERP to chemistry, and each one needs someone who knows where to look.
What I built
An MCP server with governed tool calling over those seven data sources, so a plain question becomes scoped queries and an answer that links back to its sources.
What changed
Used by 100+ people across foundry deployments.

Charge mix and production planning

MILP · optimization · planning

  1. chemistry
  2. stock
  3. capacity
  4. MILP
  5. charge plan
Problem
In non-digitized foundries, the charge mix and the melt plan are worked out by hand.
What I built
A MILP charge-mix optimizer that respects the grade's chemistry window and what is in stock, and a multi-stage planner for production on top of it.
What changed
2–5% lower melt cost and 5–8% more effective capacity, with better on-time-in-full delivery.
